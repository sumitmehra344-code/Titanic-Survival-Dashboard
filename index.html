05 // COHESION DYNAMICS Social Networks vs Isolation

Social Support Networks Provided a Structural Shield

Disaster mythology often claims that panic disintegrates social bonds into selfish individual clawing. Titanic records empirically disprove this.

Passengers traveling in family units survived at a substantially higher rate than solitary individuals, propelled by shared situational awareness, mutual assistance, and collective advocacy.

Behavioral Mechanism

Coordinated family units navigated flooding passageways together, securing lifeboat ingress far better than solo travelers.
