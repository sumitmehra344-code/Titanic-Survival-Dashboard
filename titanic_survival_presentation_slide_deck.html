03 // SOCIOECONOMIC HIERARCHY Physical Proximity & Deck Geography
2.6x ADVANTAGE: 1ST OVER 3RD CLASS

Physical Proximity Scaled Directly With Privilege

Ticket class dictated physical geography. First-class staterooms sat on Boat Deck and A/B/C decks with direct, unhindered promenade access.

Third-class steerage was located deep in the lower hull (Decks E, F, G), isolated by narrow corridors, locked security grates, and lack of alarm broadcasts.

05 // COHESION DYNAMICS Relational Support Structures
+20.2% PROBABILITY LIFT

Social Support Provided a Structural Advantage

Disaster psychology often assumes individuals in collective panic turn selfishly against each other. Titanic manifest data contradicts this assumption.

Passengers traveling with family members survived at a dramatically higher rate than solitary individuals, aided by information sharing, collective navigation, and moral resolve.

Sociological Mechanism

Coordinated search & group advocacy out-competed solitary, uninformed wandering in flooded hull labyrinths.
